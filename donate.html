Our Team

The BASF South Africa Trust places diversity and inclusion at the core of its leadership. Our Board of Trustees and team represent a broad range of backgrounds and perspectives, united in the mission to empower Black South African women through education and sustainable development. We believe a diverse, respectful, and inclusive team fosters innovation, strengthens governance, and amplifies our impact. Inclusion guides our decisions, partnerships, and strategic vision. Below you will find profiles of the Board of Trustees and key team members, each bringing distinct expertise.

Zukiswa Makunga

Designation: Trustee

Bio: Governance and trust professional with 10+ years’ experience in fiduciary oversight, stakeholder engagement, and strategic advisory roles. She serves as a BASF South Africa Trust Trustee and Advisory Board Member for Circular Energy, with expertise in compliance, financial oversight, and board-level decision-making.

Kumarasen Govender

Designation: Trustee

Bio: Mr Kumarasen Govender is an external trustee and former BASF employee with a background in chemistry and business. He has broad experience in industry and is committed to supporting youth education in science and technology.

Liz Brenner

Designation: Trustee

Bio: Biochemist and retired Wits University academic who served as Assistant Dean: Teaching and Learning. She has led key student access programmes and continues to lecture first-year students. Liz is a BASF Trust trustee, supporting students in Science and Engineering.

Nthatisi Moleko

Designation: Trustee

Bio: BASF Trust Administrator since 2023, responsible for governance, compliance, reporting, and beneficiary support. She is committed to integrity, confidentiality, and effective trust administration.

Akhona Ntsele

Designation:Trustee

Bio: Akhona Zodidi Ntsele is an experienced attorney and Trustee of the BASF South Africa Trust, dedicated to advancing higher education opportunities and empowering young women.
