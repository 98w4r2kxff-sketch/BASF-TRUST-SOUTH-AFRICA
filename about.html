What BASF South Africa Trust Does

The BASF South Africa Trust provides:

  • Scholarships, bursaries, and awards for South African Black women pursuing studies in chemistry, science, or engineering.
  • Funding and grants to recognized institutions of higher learning.
  • Support for charities and Public Benefit Organizations (PBOs) with similar objectives.

We emphasize collaborative partnerships to broaden Black economic empowerment and drive meaningful change.

Since 2018, the trust has funded students to the value of R0 spread across different Provinces in South Africa!
